About

Products from the TITO and EF5 forecast chain of the UFFIS project. Current conditions use the satellite rainfall runs (IMERG and SCaMPR). The flood forecast uses the StormLab rainfall ensemble. Flood inundation and IBF come from the flood map library of the Santa Ines Petapa pilot. Click a gauge on the map for its ensemble hydrograph, at 900 m or 90 m resolution.

University of Iowa IIHR and WMO. Basemaps: Esri, CARTO, OpenStreetMap contributors. Administrative and local layers: SEGEPLAN and INSIVUMEH datasets prepared for the UFFIS project.
